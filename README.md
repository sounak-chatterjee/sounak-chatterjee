## 📌 Executive Summary

I view biological systems as complex information networks governed by physical laws, chemical thermodynamics, and computational logic. My research interests lie at the convergence of statistical mechanics, quantum information theory, and machine learning to model complex biological phenomena. 

My long-term objectives are focused on building robust computational architectures for astrobiology, bioprocess engineering, and biomolecular simulations. I prioritize mathematical rigor, clean and reproducible code, and physical-first principles to bridge the gap between abstract computing and physical biology.
